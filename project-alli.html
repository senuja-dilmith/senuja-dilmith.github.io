Advanced Light Intensity Indicator

A hardware-focused measurement system for stable, accurate light intensity monitoring and indication.

ALLI

Project Overview

The Advanced Light Intensity Indicator was developed to improve light measurement reliability in practical electronic systems. It focused on capturing changes in illumination, stabilizing sensor signal quality, and enabling meaningful indication of ambient intensity levels.

The work combined signal interpretation, hardware sensitivity, and system-level design to deliver a robust and understandable light detection concept suitable for further embedded or automation applications.
